## Proposed information architecture

The recommended pages are Home, Services overview, detailed service pages, Services and pricing, Process and onboarding, Work or case studies, About, Contact or project brief, FAQs, and relevant policy pages. Do not create a package catalog because the owner says there are no preset packages. Create pages only when there is enough approved content for them; an empty case study or invented About story would mislead visitors.

The home page should quickly explain who the agency helps, the outcomes it works toward, service groups, genuine work evidence when supplied, pricing guidance, delivery stages, the onboarding research/design draft, separately priced ongoing support, and a clear next action. Use short summaries that lead to detailed pages rather than a single overwhelming page. Each service page should explain the problem, possible scope, what is selected for a specific project, limits, dependencies, client responsibilities, and price factors. Distinguish **available service**, **chosen one-time scope**, **separately quoted work**, and **monthly ongoing service**.

Use only verifiable portfolio examples and approved permissions. No client names, logos, testimonials, results, certifications, awards, partner badges, or statistics may be fabricated. If proof is unavailable, describe the process and scope honestly instead.

## Confirmed pricing direction; details still open

Starter Shifter sells services, not preset packages or a standalone product. The owner states a **$1,000 default one-time price** that can move up or down as the client adds or removes services. The owner also says the listed services/features are covered at that default. This expresses commercial intent; it does not yet define quantities, duration, supported platforms, custom-work limits, or third-party costs. Avoid presenting “all features for $1,000” as an unlimited, final quote. The selected scope and price should be written down for each client.

The owner says **40% advance payment and a signed contract** precede onboarding. Other payment and legal terms are not documented here yet. Maintenance or other continuing service requested after or alongside the one-time project is a separate monthly charge that varies with the client's chosen coverage. The exact monthly amount and what counts as an external or continuing service are TBD.

Before quoting or publishing a precise offer, define the selected services, outputs and quantities, setup versus recurring work, timeline, prerequisites, revision allowance, client responsibilities, exclusions, and support coverage. Terms such as taxes, remaining payment, ownership, and cancellation are intentionally deferred to [questions.md](questions.md); do not guess them.

Agency charges must be distinguished from platform subscriptions; hosting and domains; themes, apps, plugins, and extensions; ERP, CRM, chatbot, and other software subscriptions; usage charges; payment processing and transaction fees; advertising spend; asset licensing; and shipping or fulfillment charges. Third-party prices change: record a source and verification date before citing one, and recheck before publication. Separate social media management fees from ad spend.

### The 24-hour initial handoff intention

The owner says the client first signs the shared contract, pays the 40% advance, and provides details about the company/project. The client then enters onboarding and its group. The first handoff is intended at the 24-hour point and includes research on the company, market segment, and competitors, plus a basic Figma design draft with variants from which the client can choose and request revisions. The earlier wording was “within 24 hours”; the latest wording was “after 24 hours.” **The precise deadline, clock start, elapsed versus business hours, timezone, minimum input quality, research depth, number of Figma variants/revisions, complex-project exceptions, and approval to begin implementation remain TBD.** Do not publish an unconditional deadline until clarified. This does not promise a completed website, all assets, all research, or all integrations in 24 hours.

**Confirmed handoff elements:** company/market/competitor research and a basic Figma design draft with selectable variants. **Proposed additional outline, subject to approval:** goals and priorities; recommended platform and approach; agreed scope; implementation stages; milestones and responsibilities; dependencies and open questions. Research depth must match the time and inputs actually available.

## Writing and decision rules

Use specific, client-friendly language and explain technical terms when needed. Connect each service to a business need, then show what is selected, what can be added, and what the client supplies. Avoid “we handle everything,” universal compatibility, guaranteed outcomes, and unbounded support promises. Preserve confirmed facts; label recommendations and assumptions in internal work. The confirmed price reference and advance may be stated with their limits, but unresolved scope and contractual details require owner input before publication. Update this file when the owner explicitly confirms or changes a policy; move answered items from [questions.md](questions.md) into the appropriate confirmed section and note any remaining conflict.
