The recommended pages are Home, Services overview, detailed service pages, Services and pricing, Process and onboarding, Work or case studies, About, Contact or project brief, FAQs, and relevant policy pages. The owner-approved Basic/Pro comparison can appear on the homepage; additional plans need owner confirmation.
